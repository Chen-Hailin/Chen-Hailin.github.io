About

I am a researcher working on Large Language Models and LLM agents. I'm currently a Quantitative Researcher at Susquehanna International Group.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Nanyang Technological University (NTU), advised by Shafiq Joty. My research spans code generation, modular prompting, knowledge distillation, and benchmarking LLM reasoning.

Previously, I worked at Salesforce Research, YITU and Microsoft Research.

I. Basic Statistics

  • Chinchilla law: 20 tokens / param — but inference-aware scaling pushes ratio much higher [Sardana et al. 2024]
  • DeepSeek V4 Pro: 1.6T total params (49B active) · 32T training tokens
  • FLOPs/param/token = 6 (fwd×1 + bwd×2 + wupdate×2)
  • Total training FLOPs: 6 × 1.6e12 × 32e12 = 3.07e26 (307 YottaFLOPs)
  • H200 FP8 peak: 3,958 TFLOPS · MFU ≈ 40% → ~1,583 eff TFLOPS
  • ~54M GPU-hours · 100K H200 → ~23 days · ~$162M
